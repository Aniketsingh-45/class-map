Hackathon Judge & Architecture Cheat-Sheet

Class Map FLN Engine
1. 🎯 NIPUN Bharat & ASER Standard Alignment

Implements the exact 5-tier ASER ladder (Beginner ➔ Letter ➔ Word ➔ Paragraph ➔ Story) with 30-second rapid stopping rules so struggling students are not frustrated.

2. 👩‍🏫 Teacher-in-the-Loop Human Oversight

AI never acts as an unchecked gatekeeper. In homework review, the teacher can listen to student audio clips, inspect slate handwriting, manually adjust grades/levels, and provide personal guidance.

3. 🧠 Multimodal AI (SARVAM Indic STT + Claude Vision)

Devanagari text-to-speech alignment handles dialect variants (e.g., vo ➔ vah). Vision OCR reads chalk slates and classifies arithmetic error patterns (e.g., smaller-from-larger subtraction bug).

4. 🔒 Privacy by Design (DPDP Act Compliant)

First-name/roll-number only (no Aadhaar, phone numbers, or government IDs stored). Audio clips are compressed under 2MB and run securely on local school edge instances.

5. 📶 Offline-First & Resilient Local Caching

Built-in ServiceWorker/Fetch cache with simulated offline testing button. Works seamlessly even when village schools lose internet connectivity.
